Location & Practical Details

Pioneer Lodge in Red Deer

Workshops take place at Pioneer Lodge, a historic community hall in Parkvale, next to the arena and curling rink.

The lodge is wheelchair accessible, has off-street parking, and includes a kitchen with a fridge and microwave.

Open in Google Maps Open in Apple Maps Pioneer Lodge Info

Parking

Off-street parking is available. Please arrive with enough time to park, settle in, and be ready when your workshop begins.

Snacks & Tea

Light snacks and tea will be available. Please bring a water bottle, lunch, and any extra food you need for the day.

Longer Workshops

Workshops over 5 hours include a 1-hour break partway through. Bring something to store in the fridge or heat in the microwave, or head out for a bite nearby.

What to Bring

  • All weaving materials and tools are provided.
  • A water bottle, lunch, and any extra snacks you may need.
  • Comfortable clothing for a hands-on workshop.
  • Any supports your hands or body need for a full workshop day.

Children & Youth

Children over 12 are welcome in Gentle Beginner and Beginner workshops with a dedicated caregiver.

We ask for a 1:1 adult-to-child ratio, and children must be supervised by their caregiver at all times.
