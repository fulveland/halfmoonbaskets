Policies

Children & Youth

Children over 12 are welcome in Gentle Beginner and Beginner workshops with a dedicated caregiver. Intermediate workshops are generally best suited to adults or older youth with some weaving experience.

All sales are final. Tickets are non-refundable. If you are unable to attend, you may pass your ticket to a friend.
