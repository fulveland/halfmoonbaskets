November Workshop Details

Each workshop is designed as a doorway into a particular material, technique, or basket form. All weaving materials and tools are provided. Choose the date and time that works for you, then use the registration button for that offering.

Gentle Beginner · $115

Cattail Berry Basket

Friday November 13 · 9 AM–1 PM Register

What if the rustling cattails that line the marshes could be coaxed into carrying your berries home?

Join us to find out, as we transform these green blades into a small vessel for gathering summer’s sweetest moments.

In this hands-on workshop, you will learn how to weave cattails over a wooden form, practicing timeless techniques such as the checkerboard weave, pairing, and waling. Together, we will finish the basket with a simple trac border, creating a sturdy, beautiful basket made entirely from cattails.

Workshop rhythm

This is a 4-hour workshop with approximately 3 hours of guided instruction. We will take a 15-minute break partway through for tea and cake.

Skills learned

  • Preparing and handling cattail leaves
  • Weaving over a wooden form
  • Checkerboard weave and pairing
  • Basic joins
  • Finishing with a simple trac border

Beginner · $145

Bird Feeder

Friday November 13 · 1 PM–3 PM Register Friday November 13 · 3 PM–5 PM Register Sunday November 15 · 6 PM–8 PM Register

Craft a suet bird feeder from locally harvested willow. This workshop is perfect for anyone curious about weaving with natural materials who would like to begin with a simple project.

During this session, you will get to know willow and learn a foundational weave called pairing. Price includes a brick of high-quality suet so you can set it up as soon as you get home.

Workshop rhythm

This is a 2-hour workshop with 1 hour and 45 minutes of guided instruction. We will take a 15-minute break partway through for tea and cake.

Skills learned

  • How to select willow for different parts of a project
  • Using a jig to hold a form while you weave
  • Pairing
  • Basic joins
  • Trac border
  • Creating a functional garden object

Beginner · $165

Wreath Making

Friday November 13 · 5 PM–8 PM Register

Spend the evening getting to know locally harvested willow through the simple, satisfying work of bending and binding rods into wreaths.

We will practice shaping, wrapping, and securing willow while learning how tension, angle, and pressure affect the finished form.

Ticket price includes enough willow to make 5 wreaths. We will create 2 together in the workshop, and you can bring the rest of your willow home to continue practicing.

For those hoping to weave a Butt Cheek Basket on Saturday, this workshop is also an opportunity to make a custom handle and rim for your basket.

Workshop rhythm

This is a 3-hour workshop with 2 hours and 45 minutes of guided instruction. We will take a 15-minute break partway through for tea and cake.

Skills learned

  • Getting to know locally harvested willow
  • Bending rods into a balanced wreath form
  • Using tension, angle, and pressure with intention
  • Wrapping and securing willow cleanly
  • Adjusting the design to make handles and hoops for baskets

Beginner · $145

Berry Boat

Saturday November 14 · 9 AM–2 PM Register Sunday November 15 · 9 AM–1 PM Register

I love using this small tray to harvest quick snacks from my garden. Its shape is especially well suited to berries. By placing the boat beneath the branch I am harvesting from, I can easily catch any fruit that falls as my hand brushes by.

In this beginner-friendly workshop, you will learn to shape the form using a simple wooden jig, allowing the berry boat to emerge naturally as you weave.

We will begin with a short tutorial on using a small jig to weave a berry boat.

We will explore how to find and identify willow for harvesting, and how to gather it responsibly. You will also learn methods for drying, storing, and preparing willow for weaving.

After the tutorial, you can expect to spend another 3 to 4 hours filling in your boat. This is a great first project and a gentle way to begin learning the rhythm of weaving.

Workshop rhythm

This is a 4–5 hour workshop that begins with a short guided tutorial, followed by a 15-minute break for tea and cake, after which you can continue weaving at your own pace. You are welcome to stay and complete a second boat if time permits.

Skills learned

  • Flat weave
  • Working off a jig
  • Ways to support willow and prevent snapping during tight bends

Intermediate · $195

Butt Cheek Basket

Saturday November 14 · 11 AM–6 PM Register Saturday November 14 · 2 PM–9 PM Register

A melon basket, or “buttocks basket,” curves and rounds in all the right ways. Playful and practical, its form settles naturally against your hip as you wander.

We will explore how willow can be encouraged into fullness, learning the techniques that allow the form to dip, swell, and rise with intention. Watching slender rods become a rounded, sturdy vessel is one of the small joys of working with willow.

A beautiful everyday basket for wandering, gathering, and carrying what you love. If your basket grows especially cheeky, you may have a little homework to complete it.

If you are interested in creating a custom-shaped basket, join us Friday evening to make your own wreaths to use as the hoop and handle in your basket.

Workshop rhythm

This is a 7-hour workshop with 3 hours of guided instruction, followed by a 1-hour break. After the break, you can settle back into the weave and continue at your own pace.

Skills learned

  • Tying a God’s eye knot
  • Encouraging willow into a rounded melon basket form
  • Flat weave
  • Packing weave
  • Using hoops to shape the dip, swell, and rise of the basket

Beginner · $155

A small willow project for learning the feel of willow in the hands. This is a useful beginner step for those interested in learning the basics of stake-and-strand basketry.

Skills learned

  • Tying a slath base

Intermediate · $185

Tiny Trug

Sunday November 15 · 10 AM–6 PM Register

In this workshop, we will shape a tiny willow trug, a small basket made to cradle soft petals, fragrant herbs, and small gathered treasures.

This workshop is an invitation to slow down, work with your hands, and share in the quiet beauty of weaving with willow.

An approachable intermediate project for those ready to build confidence with willow while shaping a small, functional trug.

Workshop rhythm

This is an 8-hour workshop with 7 hours of guided instruction and a 1-hour break partway through.

Skills learned

  • How to use a weavers jig to hold your willow in place while you weave.
  • Ways to support willow and prevent snapping during tight bends
  • Basic Joins
  • Attaching and tying a simple functional handle

What to Bring

  • A water bottle and any snacks you may need.
  • Food for longer workshops, if you are staying through a break.
  • Comfortable clothing for hands-on making.
