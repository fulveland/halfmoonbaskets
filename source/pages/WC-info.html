Attending Weaving Connections

Weaving Connections is happening this week! Please take a moment to read this page — you'll learn everything you need to know to have a safe and enjoyable time with us.

Parking

There will be parking in the main lot in front of the greenhouses, but not enough for all of us. If you are able-bodied, please park on the road east of the greenhouse gates.

Here is the greenhouse map which shows parking areas — and be prepared for mud!

Upon Arrival

Enter through the main doors of the greenhouse. The first room is called the "Header House". Restrooms are to the right, and the kitchen is straight ahead.

The Header House is where we'll all meet up initially. There will be a table with name tags, and maps of the greenhouses & workshop locations. The Header House is also where we'll come together to enjoy our meals.

The Greenhouse

We will be weaving in a working, operational greenhouse that is just entering its busiest time of the year. The staff have done their best to make plenty of room for all the willow and weavers this weekend. However, some greenhouses still contain plants, and staff may be working around us on Friday. Feel free to look around, but please be respectful of the work being done to keep the greenhouse running.

Animals

There are three dogs and two cats on the property, all of whom are very friendly. The small black & white border collie is old, deaf, and blind, so she can be a bit nervous around new people. Please be gentle with her and avoid reaching out too quickly, as she may not see you and could get scared.

What to Wear

The temperature in the greenhouse reflects the weather outside. It can be sunny and warm, or quite chilly. Bring appropriate clothing:

  • Warm: short sleeve shirt • summer shoes or sandals • sunscreen • hat
  • Cold: a warm sweater • winter jacket • long underwear • toque • fingerless gloves • winter or mud boots

Meals

If you have purchased meals, please bring your own plate, bowl, mug and utensils. We want to create no waste, like a hike: take out what you bring, leave no trace.

If you are bringing a bag lunch, please bring ice. We do have access to a small fridge, but space is limited, and we cannot guarantee there will be room.

The microwave is on the same breaker as our caterer's slowcookers. We ask that you wait until 1:15 or 6:15 to heat your meals unless Katye gives us the go-ahead to use the microwave earlier.

There will be a community snack station! We encourage you to bring snacks, tea, coffee, and anything else that'd make you happy to share with the community — except alcohol. This is a dry event.

There is a small kitchen available for everyone to use, equipped with a sink, microwave, and kettle. The tap water is safe to drink (it's city water).

Activities

Open Weave

Take a break from classes and join us for an open weave where anyone can explore weaving and creative play. This year we will have materials for making friendship bracelets and small woven pieces using cordage, bark, cattails, dandelions, yarn, and more.

You are also welcome to bring your own materials or a project you are already working on. Open weave is simply shared time and space to sit together and make.

We will have a limited number of chairs available in the open weave area. If you have one you'd like to use for yourself, or one you wouldn't mind sharing, bring a chair.

Finally, if you have any to spare, bring an armful of willow to share. We need all the willow we can get.

Tool Making with Crys Harse

Special guest Crys Harse will be setting up a small tool-making station in the open weave area. She will guide us in making a simple threader — especially useful when working with soft materials such as cattail and bulrush.

If you would like to make a tool, please bring a small piece of root or hardwood doweling (approximately 4–6 inches long and about thumb-width) to use as a handle. Crys recommends not using willow, as the soft pith in the centre is not ideal for this type of tool.

Sharing your Weaving Adventures

Have you been busy weaving? We have many new weavers joining us this year! Bring some of your work to show off and inspire your fellow weavers.

If you'd like to exchange one of your pieces, place it on the table at the Give and Receive Station — then choose another to take home in return.

Community Library

Have a favourite weaving book? Curious what others have been reading?

We will have a small community library set up in the open weave area. Feel free to bring a book to share and spend some time browsing.

Many weaving books are out of print and quite precious. We kindly ask that all books remain in the open weave area and are returned to the library table when you are finished.

Please write your name clearly on the inside cover so books can find their way back to you.

Tools for Sale

At designated times throughout the weekend, we will have a small selection of basketry Tools for sale. You can make payment through CC, cash, or e-transfer. If you have basketry-related books, tools, or materials you would like to sell, you are welcome to sell them in the main greenhouse at this time.

Friday Night — Alberta Basketry Guild Annual General Meeting

Our Annual General Meeting takes place during Weaving Connections, and everyone is welcome to attend.

Our Annual General Meeting takes place during Weaving Connections, and everyone is welcome to attend.

This is where we pause together and look at how the Guild is doing — what we’ve built, what we’ve learned, and what we’re tending next.

During the meeting, we will:

  • Share a short President’s Report
  • Review the Guild’s finances
  • Hear brief updates from committees
  • Talk about plans for the coming year
  • Hold elections for Board positions as needed

All are welcome to listen and take part in the conversation. Voting takes place in person and is open to current Guild members, in accordance with our bylaws. Nominations may be made from the floor.

If you have ideas, energy, or skills you would like to offer, this is also a time to step forward.

We will have a sign-up sheet available for those interested in joining a committee or helping with specific projects in the coming year. You do not need to take on a large role — many hands carrying small pieces is how this Guild continues.

Not a member yet? The best time to register is before the AGM — so register now!

After you register, please take 15 minutes to fill out the Members' Survey.

We look forward to seeing you there!
