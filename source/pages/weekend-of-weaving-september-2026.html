Gentle Beginner

Random Weave with Bulrush

$115

Friday, September 25 10 AM–2 PM Register Saturday, September 26 9 AM–1 PM Register Saturday, September 26 10 AM–2 PM Register Sunday, September 27 10 AM–2 PM Register

Wild or random weaving is a playful way to begin working with natural materials, letting the basket take shape through curiosity, movement, and touch.

In this hands-on workshop, you will explore bulrush and its soft, flexible nature as we coax loose strands into a small sculptural vessel.

We will talk about where bulrush grows, how it is harvested and prepared, and how to work with the material as it bends, twists, and settles into form.

By the end of the session, you will have created your own wild-woven bulrush basket to take home, along with a deeper feel for weaving with soft wetland plants.

Skills learned

  • Getting to know bulrush as a soft wetland weaving material
  • Starting and building a wild or random weave form
  • Adding structure while following the natural movement of the material
  • Creating a small wild-woven bulrush basket

Workshop rhythm

4 hours · 1 hour guided instruction · 15-minute break for light snacks and tea

Gentle Beginner

Random Weave with Willow Bark

$145

Friday, September 25 12 PM–4 PM Register Saturday, September 26 12 PM–4 PM Register

Wild or random weaving is a fun, easy way to get into weaving with natural materials.

During this workshop, you will be invited to explore willow bark and its unique, leather-like qualities.

We will discuss how to find and identify willow bark for harvesting, and how to harvest it responsibly. We will also cover different methods for drying, storing, and preparing willow bark for weaving.

By the end of the session, you’ll have created your own wild-woven willow bark bowl to take home, along with a deeper understanding of the materials and methods used in basket weaving.

Skills learned

  • Identifying and responsibly harvesting willow bark
  • Drying, storing, and preparing willow bark for weaving
  • Exploring wild or random weaving with soft strip materials
  • Creating a wild-woven willow bark bowl

Workshop rhythm

4 hours · 1 hour guided instruction · 15-minute break for light snacks and tea

Gentle Beginner

Cattail Berry Basket

$115

Friday, September 25 2 PM–6 PM Register Saturday, September 26 6 PM–10 PM Register

What if the rustling cattails that line the marshes could be coaxed into carrying your berries home?

Join us to find out, as we transform these green blades into a small vessel for gathering summer’s sweetest moments.

In this hands-on workshop, you will learn how to weave cattails over a wooden form, practicing timeless techniques such as the checkerboard weave, pairing, and waling. Together, we will finish the basket with a simple trac border, creating a sturdy, beautiful berry basket made entirely from cattails.

Skills learned

  • Preparing and handling cattail leaves
  • Weaving over a wooden form
  • Checkerboard weave and pairing
  • Finishing with a simple trac border

Workshop rhythm

4 hours · 3 hours guided instruction · 15-minute break for light snacks and tea

Beginner

Twill Berry Basket with Willow Bark

$145

Friday, September 25 6 PM–10 PM Register Saturday, September 26 2 PM–6 PM Register

A small berry basket built through twill, a rhythm of over-under weaving that comes together almost like a woven textile. Willow bark is the material here, leather-like and pleasantly flexible in the hand.

This one is still beginner-friendly, but the twill pattern asks a little more attention than a plain weave, since you’ll want to keep track of where you are in the sequence as you go.

Skills learned

  • Identifying and responsibly harvesting willow bark
  • Drying, storing, and preparing willow bark for weaving
  • Following a simple twill pattern
  • Keeping tension even around a small form
  • Finishing a lightweight bark basket

Workshop rhythm

4 hours · 3 hours guided instruction · 15-minute break for light snacks and tea

Beginner

Willow Berry Basket

$185

Sunday, September 27 12 PM–8 PM Register

Six inches wide and round, this little basket carries all the fundamentals of stake-and-strand work. It is small enough to hold in one hand and complete enough to teach you a great deal — to my mind, the best of the beginner projects.

You will lay a slath base, then move through waling, pairing, and slewing to build the sides, finishing with a rod border, with an optional wrapped-core handle if you like. Willow asks for some hand and finger strength, and this is a kind place to begin — no experience is necessary.

Skills learned

  • Tying a slath base
  • Waling and pairing up the sides
  • Slewing for a fuller wall
  • Finishing with a rod border
  • Adding an optional wrapped-core handle

Workshop rhythm

8 hours · 1-hour break · includes light snacks and tea
