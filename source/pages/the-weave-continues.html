Thank you for weaving alongside us.

This page is a place to arrive after our time together, somewhere between what we shared and what will keep unfolding in your own hands. It holds a few points of orientation, some gentle reminders, and pathways into the wider weaving community.

Basketry is not learned all at once. It reveals itself slowly, through time, attention, and return.

You are now part of a long, living lineage of hands and plants, places and stories. May this page keep you in quiet conversation with the work, the land, and the community that holds it.
