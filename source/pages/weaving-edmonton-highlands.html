Three Workshops · One Highlands Home

A closer look

All the depth on each day: what you will make, what you will learn, and how the hours run. Materials and tools are provided, and payment is by e-transfer once you reserve.

Sunday, December 13 · Intermediate · $185

Tiny Trug

Sunday, December 13 10 AM–5 PM Reserve

A tiny willow trug, made to cradle soft petals, fragrant herbs, and the small treasures gathered from the forest floor.

We will spend a cosy Sunday slowing down with the willow, bending sticks and building a little basket by hand. This is an approachable intermediate project that builds confidence with the material. Keen beginners are welcome, though it is a full day of weaving and your hands will know it by the end.

Skills learned

  • Reading and working a willow rod
  • Shaping a small trug
  • A woven wall and border
  • Finishing and caring for willow

The day

About seven hours, with a break for chai and cake.

Saturday, January 23 · Intermediate · $185

Berry Basket

Saturday, January 23 9 AM–5 PM Reserve

In this hands-on workshop, you will be invited into the gentle rhythm of weaving as you create a small round willow basket, a beautiful introduction for anyone beginning their basketry journey.

You will learn foundational stake-and-strand techniques: a slath base, waling, pairing, slewing, and a rod border, with an optional wrapped-core handle.

Skills learned

  • Tying a slath base
  • Waling and pairing
  • Slewing for a fuller wall
  • A rod border
  • An optional wrapped-core handle

The day

A full day, about eight hours, with a break for chai and cake.

Saturday, March 13 · Beginner · $145

Weaving Moons & Stars

Saturday, March 13 10 AM–4 PM Reserve

Long before clocks, willow and the moon and the stars were working companions, marking the turn of the seasons. In this beginner-friendly workshop you will weave a willow moon and a star of your own.

Skills learned

  • Shaping willow into moons and stars
  • Joining willow
  • Finding and harvesting willow responsibly
  • Preparing willow for weaving

The day

About six hours, with a break for chai and cake.

What to Bring

  • All weaving materials and tools are provided.
  • A water bottle and a lunch, since these run through the day.
  • Comfortable clothing for hands-on making.
  • Any supports your hands or body need for a full day.
