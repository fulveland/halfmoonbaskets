What I bring

All materials and tools, and enough of both for every seat. Every stick used in my workshops is harvested by me, either from the wild or from willow I am helping to grow.

What you provide

A room with tables and chairs, water, and a way for people to register. Workshops run from three hours to two full days.

Group sizes

Most workshops run from six to twenty. The smallest projects take four, the largest gatherings up to thirty. Each listing gives its own range.

Where I travel

I am based near Ponoka, in central Alberta, and can teach within roughly 150 km of home for most of the year. The rest depends on the season, because the season decides where I am harvesting.

January, May, June, September, October
Travelling across Alberta. These are the months to ask about if you are further afield.
November, December, February, March, April
Harvesting near Calgary and Edmonton, and available to teach near those cities.
