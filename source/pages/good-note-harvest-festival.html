The Community Weave

Everyone adds a rod

This is not a sit-down workshop. It is a large willow sculpture that grows all day, as people wander over from the music, add a few rods, and drift back again. Come by whenever you like, and stay for two minutes or two hours.

Everyone finds a place at it. Children, first-timers, and old hands all weave side by side, and no experience is needed, just a willingness to bend a rod. Last year the crowd wove Martina the goat. This year, we are growing a flower.

Last Year at Good Note

We wove Martina the goat

Over one long golden day, a pile of willow became a life-size goat, built by whoever stopped to weave. Here is how she came together, from the first frame to her glowing candle eyes after dark.
